<objective>
Build the FEEL evaluator core: the `evaluate(&FeelExpression, &Scope) -> Value` dispatch, the flat `Scope` type, and the private `cmp` module with three-valued-logic operators (`feel_equals`, `feel_compare`, `and_3vl`, `or_3vl`, `not_3vl`) — the single most important correctness property of the phase (ROADMAP Pitfall 1).

Output: `engine/src/dmn/feel/eval.rs` with `Scope`, private `cmp` module, `evaluate` skeleton (Literal/Name/QualifiedName/AtLiteral/Range/BinOp comparison+boolean arms real; arithmetic/control/boxed arms stubbed to `Value::Null`), MANDATORY DMN-12 conformance unit tests.
