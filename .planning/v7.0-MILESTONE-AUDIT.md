## Root-Cause Note

This milestone's gaps are largely **process gaps**, not just scope gaps:
- Phases 37 and 38 were marked COMPLETE in their SUMMARY files and committed **without a VERIFICATION.md**, so the verify-work gate never ran — the dead-code cache, the falsely-complete T3, and the unmeasured perf targets all survived because nothing checked them.
- Phase 37 SUMMARY claims performance targets that were never benchmarked.
- The Phase 36 regression-gate "fix" (commit `26789d397`) did not actually make the gate functional; its stale verification still reads as the source of truth.

Restoring the verify-work gate (VERIFICATION.md for 37 and 38) and re-verifying 36 is a prerequisite to closing v7.0.
